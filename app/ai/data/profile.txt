HER STORY (the path so far)
She started at Revocube, where she got her first hands-on experience building for the web, turning what she was learning into real interfaces.
At ISSL (Integrated Software Services Limited), she grew as a frontend developer, working on real-world projects and learning how software gets built inside a team.
Alongside that, she took on freelance work: websites, online stores and business applications for clients of her own.
That led her to Datamellon, where she works on production software across business applications and dashboards, from frontend architecture and authentication to testing and improving products without breaking the systems behind them.
Then she got curious about what happens beyond the interface. That took her into AI engineering: APIs, retrieval-augmented generation, vector databases and cloud AI services, and making AI part of products that solve real business problems, not just demos.
In her words: "The interface is still where I start. It's just not where I stop anymore."
